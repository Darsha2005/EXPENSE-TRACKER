📱 How to Copy GPay SMS

On Android:
1. Open Messages app → Search "GPay" or "Google Pay"
2. Long-press an SMS → tap Copy
3. Paste into the text area on the left

Sample SMS format (supported):
• Rs.500 debited from A/c XX1234... UPI:GPay... Info: Swiggy
• ₹1000 sent to Rahul via Google Pay
• Credited Rs.200 to your account via UPI
• Dear UPI user A/C XX5678 debited by 300.00 on date... trf to merchant Ref No...

💡 Tip: You can paste many SMS messages at once — the parser handles all of them together!
